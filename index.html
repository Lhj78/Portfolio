Projects

Prenatal Nutrition AI

Machine learning, computer vision, Streamlit

A multimodal fetal nutrition assessment system for rural Indian healthcare workers. It classifies nutrition status as Normal, Mild, Moderate or Severe, gives a 0–100 risk score and suggests referral advice.

What I built

  • Trained and benchmarked Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, Random Forest, LightGBM, SVM and MLP models on maternal clinical and dietary data.
  • Used CTG, NHANES, NFHS-5 and FAO/WHO GIFT India datasets, with JSON-based explainability.
  • Fine-tuned a ResNet-50 CNN for fetal head-circumference regression on 999 HC18 ultrasound images.

How it comes together

  • Fused the image model with the tabular model into one assessment.
  • Delivered INTERGROWTH-21st growth-percentile charts and recommendations.
  • Packaged everything in a Streamlit web app that healthcare workers can use directly.
